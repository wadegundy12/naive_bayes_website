A quick naming note. Ordinary Naive Bayes is generative. “Discriminative Naive Bayes” means fitting a Naive Bayes–structured model with a conditional objective. Here, logistic regression illustrates the closely related discriminative approach; it is not another name for Naive Bayes.
01 / The mental model

Explain the data.
Or predict the label.

Both answer the same question: given an input x, which label y should we predict? What differs is the probability they fit during training.

Generative / Naive Bayes

“How would this class produce these features?”

Class y→Features x→Bayes’ rule
p(x, y) = p(y) ∏ⱼ p(xⱼ | y)

Learn class frequencies and feature probabilities within each class. Then invert the direction with Bayes’ rule to obtain p(y | x).

Generative means a probability model of inputs and labels. Sampling from email Naive Bayes produces feature vectors, not necessarily coherent sentences.

Discriminative / conditional training

“Which class fits these observed features?”

Features x→Class probability
p(y = 1 | x) = σ(b + wᵀx)

Fit the probability of the correct label directly. Logistic regression learns weights that reduce conditional log loss.

σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e⁻ᶻ) maps any score to a probability. A conditional model alone does not specify p(x).

04 / Experiment three

When evidence
echoes itself.

Naive Bayes assumes features are independent given the class. A perfect duplicate breaks that assumption: it repeats information instead of adding information.

THE DUPLICATE FEATURE TRAP03

One signal. Multiple copies.

Use a 50% spam prior. A particular signal appears in 80% of spam and 20% of normal emails. We observe the signal, then add exact copies of it as extra features.

What about discriminative learning? A retrained, unregularized logistic model can distribute the same total weight across duplicate features. It need not multiply their evidence. Regularization and training details can change this behavior; discrimination does not automatically solve every dependence problem.

Why can Naive Bayes have the same form as logistic regression?

For binary features, take the log of the ratio p(y=1 | x) / p(y=0 | x). The product of feature probabilities becomes a sum. The result is b + Σ wⱼxⱼ. Converting log-odds back to probability gives a sigmoid. Shared-variance Gaussian classes yield linear log-odds too; unequal variances generally yield a quadratic expression.

What exactly is discriminative training of Naive Bayes?

Keep a Naive Bayes parameterization, but choose its parameters to maximize Σ log p(yᵢ | xᵢ), where p(y | x) is obtained by normalizing its joint model. Ordinary generative training instead maximizes Σ log p(xᵢ, yᵢ). The identity log p(x,y) = log p(y | x) + log p(x) explains why the objectives differ: joint training also rewards explaining inputs.

Does “generative” mean the same thing as a generative AI chatbot?

The broad idea is related: model a data distribution so you can sample from it. Naive Bayes is a very simple generative model. Its independent word-feature samples do not have the language structure of a modern text generator.
